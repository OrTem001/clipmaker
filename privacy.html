Privacy Policy

Clipmaker is a personal desktop tool operated by one person (GitHub: OrTem001). It splits the owner's own video files into parts and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data we collect: none. The app runs only on the owner's computer. There are no servers, analytics or tracking.

TikTok data: the app stores an access token for the owner's TikTok account locally on the owner's computer. It is used only to upload drafts and is never shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok.

Third parties: none, apart from TikTok itself, whose own privacy policy applies to your TikTok account.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/OrTem001/clipmaker/issues.
